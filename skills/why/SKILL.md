# Explain the Evidence

Identify the exact decision or claim. Retrieve the athlete evidence used for it
and the smallest relevant set of knowledge chapters. Distinguish peer-reviewed
evidence, documented coaching frameworks, athlete-specific observations, derived
analysis, and hypotheses. When credible sources disagree, explain the practical
difference, assumptions, and relevance to this athlete. Do not invent citations
or attribute a precise threshold without a source. End with what the evidence
changes in practice and what remains uncertain.

Use the same athlete files and provider calls that supported the original
decision. If they were never retrieved or are stale, retrieve them before claiming
they support the explanation.
