# Longitudinal Memory and Privacy Policy

Use this policy whenever reading private athlete files or writing durable memory.

## Memory Authority

The private athlete repository authorizes routine local notes and checkpoints.
Activity memory is enabled unless `athlete/profile.md` disables it; this authorizes
activity notes, indexes, and summaries. Backfills, expanded retention, deletion,
Git commits, and remote pushes require explicit athlete authorization. Ask the
athlete to choose the first backfill period.

## What to Preserve

Label stored content as provider fact, athlete report, derived metric, decision, or
hypothesis, with date and source. Keep workout-specific evidence in its activity
note. Promote a pattern to `athlete/notes.md` only with dated support, source IDs,
confidence, and a review date. A confirmed preference or decision may be recorded
after one explicit athlete statement.

Preserve contextual conflicts instead of forcing one global preference. Supersede
stale memory explicitly and honor correction or deletion. During weekly reviews,
block transitions, race debriefs, and setback recovery, review unresolved
hypotheses, stale constraints, completed goals, and contradictory evidence.

After an accepted plan, health decision, race analysis, or external write, update
`athlete/checkpoint.md` with the date, active goal, decisions, pending actions,
open questions, and evidence needed next. On resume, verify current provider state
before acting on historical plans.

## Activity Memory

After activity-related work, follow `agents/activity-memory.md` once. Archive
reviewed workouts when activity memory is enabled. Maintain
`athlete/activities/index.md` and monthly summaries so retrieval does not require
scanning every record. Complete decision-critical work before synchronization.

Do not persist routes, raw streams, or third-party social content by default.
Private-file reads may transmit content to the model provider. Retrieve only what
the decision needs and never expose unrelated health, location, identity, or
third-party data.
