# Training Glossary

Use plain language with the athlete. Introduce an acronym on first use only when
it helps interpret provider data or training literature.

| Term | Acronym | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Chronic training load | CTL | A weighted estimate of longer-term training load, commonly calculated over about six weeks. It is a model output, not fitness itself. |
| Acute training load | ATL | A weighted estimate of recent training load, commonly calculated over about one week. It is one view of fatigue, not a direct measurement. |
| Training stress balance | TSB | CTL minus ATL, often labeled form. Interpret it with symptoms, history, and the model's settings. |
| Training stress score | TSS | A provider-specific estimate combining workout duration and intensity. |
| Heart-rate variability | HRV | Variation in time between heartbeats. Compare consistent within-person measurements and trends rather than isolated values. |
| Resting heart rate | RHR | Heart rate measured at rest. Changes can reflect recovery, illness, stress, environment, and measurement conditions. |
| Aerobic threshold | AeT | The upper boundary of predominantly low-intensity aerobic work; definitions and measurement methods vary. |
| Anaerobic or lactate threshold | AnT or LT | A high sustainable intensity near the transition to rapidly accumulating fatigue; definitions and tests vary. |
| Lactate-threshold heart rate | LTHR | Heart rate associated with an athlete's estimated lactate threshold. |
| Aerobic deficiency syndrome | ADS | An Uphill Athlete term for a large gap between aerobic and anaerobic thresholds. Treat it as a coaching framework, not a diagnosis. |
| Muscular endurance | ME | The ability of muscles to sustain repeated force under fatigue, especially during long climbs and descents. |
| Rate of perceived exertion | RPE | The athlete's subjective effort rating, usually on a 1-10 scale. |
| Functional threshold power | FTP | An estimate of the highest power sustainable for roughly an hour; exact protocols vary. |
| Vertical gain | Vert | Total climbing accumulated during an activity or training period. |
| Did not start / did not finish | DNS / DNF | A race entry not started, or a race started but not completed. |
