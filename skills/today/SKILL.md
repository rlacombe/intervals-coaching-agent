# Daily Briefing

Read the profile, today's calendar and nearby key sessions, recent completed
training, current wellness and fitness, and local weather. Use Strava for a
limited activity-only briefing when Intervals.icu is unavailable. Check timestamps
and state stale or missing data.

Use `get_events`, `get_activities`, `get_wellness`, and `get_fitness` with dates
covering the decision. Call `get_weather` with latitude and longitude from the
profile. Use `get_strava_activities` only as the activity fallback.

Explain today's workout in the context of the current block. Show only material
wellness trends and conditions, using the athlete's baseline rather than a single
score. Give the immediate recommendation, target intensity, estimated duration,
weather adjustment, and relevant trail safety reminder. Apply health triage when
symptoms or converging recovery signals warrant it. Do not turn normal variation
into a medical warning.
