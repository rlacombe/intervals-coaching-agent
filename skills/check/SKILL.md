# Health and Load Review

## Trigger
Use for a deeper readiness, overtraining, progression, or injury-risk review.

## Minimum evidence
Read profile, health history, and relevant notes. Retrieve at least 42 complete
days of activities and load for chronic-load analysis, the matching planned
calendar, and available wellness. Use a shorter window only for an explicitly
shorter claim. With Strava alone, limit conclusions to recorded activities and
athlete reports.

Use `get_activities`, `get_fitness`, and `get_wellness` with explicit date ranges.
Use `get_events` for the matching plan. Retrieve a single activity or selected
streams only when the review requires that resolution.

## Knowledge
Retrieve data interpretation plus only the relevant recovery, injury,
age-and-sex, sleep, or volume chapter.

## Analysis
Calculate weekly time or distance, vertical gain, intensity or load, recovery
weeks, and material plan-versus-actual differences. Compare wellness signals with
the athlete's own baseline. Separate provider facts, athlete reports, derived
metrics, and hypotheses. Do not diagnose overtraining or injury from a score,
fixed threshold, or isolated reading.

## Output
Lead with the decision the athlete faces. Show dated evidence and material
calculations, then apply the health triage. Recommend the least restrictive safe
action, alternatives, and a reassessment trigger. State missing data and how it
limits the conclusion. Store only durable, sourced patterns.
