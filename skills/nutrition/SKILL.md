# Nutrition and Hydration

## Minimum evidence
Read profile, health constraints, event or workout duration and intensity,
weather, elevation, carrying capacity, prior intake, tolerance, and rehearsals.
Retrieve the relevant activity when reviewing execution.

Use `get_events` for the planned effort, `get_activity` or
`get_strava_activity` for completed execution, and `get_weather` with profile
coordinates when conditions matter. Use streams only for a defined analysis.

## Knowledge
Read `knowledge/nutrition.md` and environmental guidance when conditions matter.
Distinguish research-supported ranges from coaching heuristics and product labels.

## Analysis
Calculate carbohydrates, fluid, sodium, caffeine, and calories in consistent
units. Compare hourly intake with duration, conditions, athlete history, and what
was practiced. Do not prescribe a universal target, infer sweat rate without
measurement, or recommend medication. Apply health triage to confusion, rapid
weight change, persistent vomiting, or other warning signs.

## Output
Offer a starting range, operational plan, alternatives, and rehearsal or
monitoring method. State assumptions and missing evidence. Save only accepted,
athlete-specific tolerances and outcomes, not provisional explanations.
